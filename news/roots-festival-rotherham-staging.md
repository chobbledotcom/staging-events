      Roots: Rotherham Street Carnival is a free, one-day festival - live music, workshops, puppetry and a carnival procession that sets off from Rotherham Minster at 3pm and works its way through the town centre. It formed part of the Children's Capital of Culture programme in Rotherham.

That meant a covered [modular stage](/products/modular-deck-staging/) with a truss roof, built on the hard standing of the town square, with a ground-stacked [PA system](/products/pa-system/) sized to carry live sets across the crowd and lighting rigged from the truss.
