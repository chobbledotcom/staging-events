This is a living reference. Update it when:
- New stats become available (participant numbers, years trading, review count)
- New charity clients come on board

- Sustainability metrics change (plastic prevented, CO2 saved)

### Key Principles for Using Selling Points:

1. **Always verify stats are current** — the 25+ years figure, participant numbers, and sustainability metrics should be updated as time passes
2. **Named clients add credibility** — Alzheimer's Society, Prostate Cancer UK, and venue names are more powerful than generic "charity clients"
3. **Specific numbers beat vague claims** — "25,000 sq ft" is more credible than "large warehouse", "110,000 walkers" is more powerful than "major charity events"
4. **Insurance figures matter to charity and council procurement** — £5M PLI is a key differentiator and decision factor
5. **In-house ownership is underused** — this addresses a real pain point (subcontractor unreliability) that competitors likely can't match
6. **Named brands (Intelistage, Instagrid, Litec) are gold** — specifics over abstractions is the brand voice, and it's also the strongest SEO signal
7. **Frame USPs for charity event managers, not consumers** — they care about reliability, documentation, single-point accountability, and budget stretching
8. **Client pages are EEAT gold** — Alzheimer's Society Memory Walk with real numbers is one of the strongest trust signals available
