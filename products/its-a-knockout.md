It's a Knockout is one of our most popular corporate team building events. Teams compete across a range of inflatable and physical challenges in 3 hours of non-stop action and fun.

### What's Included

- Choice of available activities
- Event design and PA system
- Professional MC and event management
- All event equipment
- Winners medals for the winning team
- £5 million public liability insurance

### Available Activities

- Bish and Bash
- Up and Over Wedge
- Mega Assault Course
- Tunnel Vision
- Hurdles
- Scramble Net

### Family Fun Run Option

A newer format that incorporates fundraising through walking or running over inflatable obstacles. Participants must be 5 years or older.
