The Batak reaction game is one of the UK's most popular exhibition games, and we hold one of the largest hire stocks in the country. With over 25 years supplying events for brands including Amazon, Specsavers, local councils and professional sports teams.

The premise is simple but competitive: players must hit bright LED targets as they illuminate in rapid random patterns, testing reaction speed, hand-eye coordination and concentration under pressure. Each game lasts approximately 30 seconds, which means dozens of players can take part every hour, which makes it well suited to busy exhibition floors.

### Available Models

- **Batak Pro** - From £495. The full-size professional model featuring 12 bright LED targets that light up in random patterns. Scores are displayed on LED screens. The large format makes a striking centrepiece for exhibition stands and corporate events
- **Batak Lite** - From £395. A compact reaction game with 8 LED numbered targets under microcomputer control. Selectable programs offer random or repetitive sequences to suit different skill levels and event formats. Hits and misses are timed and scored on LED displays. The smaller footprint makes it ideal for tighter exhibition stands, schools, team building days and product launches

### Custom Branding

Custom branded graphics are available. For the Batak Pro, options include a full back panel (from £99), all 12 branded buttons (from £49), or a complete package with both back panel and buttons (from £125). For the Batak Lite, back panels are available from £79, with eight branded buttons and background from £149. Our design team can work with your brand guidelines.
